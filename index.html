Emoji Flix Privacy Policy

Effective 2 November 2026

In short: Emoji Flix has no accounts, and your game progress stays on your device. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device data to do that.

This policy explains how the Emoji Flix game for Android ("the app", "we") handles your information.

Information we do not collect

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. The app has no accounts and no sign-in, and we do not run servers that receive your game data.

Information stored on your device

The app saves the following on your device so that the game works:

  • your level, score and pack progress
  • your achievements and game statistics
  • your settings, such as sound, vibration and reminders

This information is not sent to us.

Advertising

The app shows banner, full-screen and optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information from your device, including:

  • your device's advertising ID
  • your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location
  • device and app information, such as model, operating system and language
  • ad activity, such as which ads were shown or tapped, and diagnostic data

Google handles this information under its own policies. You can read Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

You can limit personalized ads or delete your advertising ID in your Android device settings, and manage ad preferences in My Ad Center.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules a weekly reminder on your device when you have not played for a while. The reminder is created on your device, and no information is sent to us. You can turn it off in the app's Settings or in your Android settings.

Sharing

If you choose to share your result or use Ask a friend, Android opens the app you pick, and that app handles what you send. Nothing is shared unless you choose to share it.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • To delete your game data, use Reset all progress in Settings, or uninstall the app.
  • To stop the app's data collection, uninstall the app.

We cannot view or delete information held by Google, so please use Google's privacy tools linked above for that information.

Children

Emoji Flix is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The latest version will always be on this page, with a new effective date.
